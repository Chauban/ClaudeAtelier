## 冷知识

芬達汽水系二戰嗰陣喺納粹德國出世——可口可樂德國子公司因為禁運攞唔到糖漿，老闆 Max Keith 就用晒當時德國僅剩嘅材料：甜菜糖、乳清（整芝士嘅副產品）、榨汁剩低嘅蘋果渣，整咗隻新汽水，仲笑佢係「剩低嘢嘅剩低嘢」。改名嗰陣 Keith 叫班員工「用吓想像力」（德文 Fantasie），推銷員 Joe Knipp 即刻嗌「Fanta！」，個名就咁嚟；1943 年喺德國賣咗 300 萬箱，好多人唔係拎嚟飲，而係用嚟煮餸加甜，因為當時糖要配給。

## 背景

*（不印在卡面上，只用来帮渲染决定构图与意象。）*

今天全球賣的橙色芬達，跟二戰德國那個原版完全是兩種東西。原版是可口可樂德國子公司在禁運斷供糖漿後，老闆 Max Keith 就地取材湊出來的——材料是甜菜糖、乳清（牛奶凝結過濾後剩的液體）和榨蘋果汁剩下的果渣，他自己形容是「剩下來的剩下來」（leftovers of leftovers），那時的飲料很可能是渾濁的棕色液體。取名字時他讓員工「用用想像力」（Fantasie），推銷員 Joe Knipp 脫口而出「Fanta！」。1943 年在德國賣了 300 萬箱，很多人不是拿來喝，而是倒進湯和燉菜裡當甜味劑，因為糖實行配給；今天的橙色芬達要到 1955 年才在意大利那不勒斯重新配方。畫面感：戰時柏林郊外的農舍裡，工人往玻璃瓶裡灌著渾濁的棕色汽水。

   - 来源：<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fanta>
   - 原句：Fanta originated in Germany as a Coca-Cola alternative in 1940 due to the American trade embargo of Nazi Germany which affected the availability of Coca-Cola ingredients.
2. 發明者是可口可樂德國子公司負責人 Max Keith；原料包括甜菜糖、乳清和蘋果渣。
   - 来源：<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fanta>
   - 原句：To circumvent this, Max Keith, the head of Coca-Cola Deutschland (Coca-Cola GmbH), decided to create a new product for the German market, using only ingredients available in Germany at the time, including sugar beet, whey, and apple pomace.
3. Max Keith 形容這批原料是「leftovers of leftovers」（剩下來東西的剩下來東西）。
   - 来源：<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fanta>
   - 原句：He later described them as the "leftovers of leftovers".
4. 名字來自德文「Fantasie」（想像力），推銷員 Joe Knipp 喊出「Fanta!」。
   - 来源：<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fanta>
   - 原句：The name was the result of a brainstorming session, which started with Keith's exhorting his team to "use their imagination" (Fantasie in German), to which one of his salesmen, Joe Knipp, retorted "Fanta!".
5. 1943 年在德國賣出 300 萬箱；很多瓶子不是拿來喝，而是當煮食甜味劑，因為糖嚴重配給。
   - 来源：<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fanta>
   - 原句：In 1943, 3 million cases of Fanta were sold in Germany. Many bottles were not consumed as a beverage but used as a cooking ingredient to add sweetness and flavor to soups and stews, as sugar was severely rationed.
6. （交叉印證）原料是其他食品工業的剩餘物：果屑、蘋果纖維與果渣、甜菜糖，以及乳清——牛奶凝結過濾後剩下的液體。
   - 来源：<https://www.atlasobscura.com/articles/fanta-soda-origins-nazi-germany>
   - 原句：It was basically made from the leftovers of other food industries: fruit shavings, apple fibers and pulp, beet sugar, and whey, the liquid remaining after milk has been curdled and strained during cheese production.
7. （交叉印證）取名時 Keith 叫團隊用想像力，Joe Knipp 提出「Fanta」，即德文「fantasy」的簡稱。
   - 来源：<https://www.atlasobscura.com/articles/fanta-soda-origins-nazi-germany>
   - 原句：To name this concoction, Keith told his team to use their imagination. Joe Knipp, a salesman, pitched "Fanta," shorthand for the German word for "fantasy." It stuck.
8. （交叉印證）芬達用蘋果纖維（榨蘋果汁剩下的渣）和乳清（芝士製造的副產品）製成，1943 年賣出 300 萬箱。
   - 来源：<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Keith>
   - 原句：Keith developed a fruit flavored drink made from apple fiber, left over from cider pressings, and whey, a byproduct from cheese manufacture, creating Fanta. The drink sold three million cases in 1943, sustaining the firm's business in Germany.
