## 冷知识

The narrowest house in the world is Keret House in Warsaw — just 92 cm at its thinnest point and 152 cm at its widest, squeezed into a floor area of only 14 m² between two existing buildings. Completed in 2012 by architect Jakub Szczęsny, it is officially classed as an art installation because it does not meet Polish building codes. （世界上最窄的房子是华沙的「凯雷特之家」——最窄处仅 92 厘米，最宽处 152 厘米，挤在两栋既有建筑之间、楼面面积只有 14 平方米。它由建筑师雅库布·什琴斯尼于 2012 年建成，因不符合波兰建筑规范，被官方归类为一件艺术装置。）

## 背景

*（不印在卡面上，只用来帮渲染决定构图与意象。）*

The record belongs to a real sliver of a home: a two-storey steel-and-glass box with a white-painted interior, wedged into the gap between a pre-war house and an apartment block at 22 Chłodna and 74 Żelazna streets in Warsaw. Inside, a ladder stands in for the stairs, the fridge holds only two drinks, and there are no openable windows — daylight comes through translucent glass panels, and the retractable entry stairs fold away into the living area. Its first resident, Israeli writer Etgar Keret, said staying there felt like "a memorial to my family," because his parents' families died under the Nazi occupation of Poland during World War II; in 2019 the house was named one of the world's most iconic homes.
