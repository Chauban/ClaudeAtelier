## 冷知识

Founded in 1521 by the merchant Jakob Fugger, the Fuggerei in Augsburg, Germany, is the world's oldest social housing complex still in use: its 67 houses still charge an annual rent of one Rhenish guilder (€0.88), unchanged for five centuries. In return, residents must be Catholic, say three daily prayers for the founder's family — and the gates are still locked every night at 10 PM.（德国奥格斯堡的富格社区由商人雅各布·富格于1521年创立，是全世界仍在使用的历史最悠久的社会福利住房：67栋房屋的年租金至今仍是1莱茵古尔登（约0.88欧元），五百年来从未变过；作为回报，住户须为创始人家族每天祈祷三次，而社区大门每晚10点依然准时落锁。）
