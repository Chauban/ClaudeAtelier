## 背景

*（不印在卡面上，只用来帮渲染决定构图与意象。）*

出自美國 Fairfield 大學心理學教授 Linda Henkel 2014 年發表喺《Psychological Science》嘅論文（25(2):396-402，題目 "Point-and-Shoot Memories"）：實驗喺大學校內嘅 Bellarmine 美術館進行——大學生參加導覽，一部分展品指定影相、另一部分淨係睇；第二日考記憶，影過相嗰啲展品記得又少又模糊。反轉位：放大特寫某個局部嚟影，唔單止嗰部分、連畫面外嘅部分記憶都保得住（特寫逼大腦投入更多注意同加工）；Henkel 將相機比喻做『外部記憶裝置』，認為大腦諗住「相機會記住」就唔再加工，佢嘅名言係 "People so often whip out their cameras almost mindlessly to capture a moment, to the point that they are missing what is happening right in front of them." 畫面感：美術館入面人人舉住手機對住名畫咔咔咔，打卡文化、旅行全程影相都係呢個現象——金句正好點題。

   - 来源：<https://www.bbc.com/future/article/20150901-are-you-taking-too-many-pictures>
   - 原句：In her study from 2014, students were led on a museum tour and asked to take photos of certain works of art – and only observe others.
3. 第二日考記憶，影過相嗰啲展品記得嘅細節差咗
   - 来源：<https://www.bbc.com/future/article/20150901-are-you-taking-too-many-pictures>
   - 原句：When they were tested the next day, they were less able to remember details of objects that they had photographed.
4. 結果：影低成件展品，認得嘅展品數量同細節都少過淨係睇嗰啲（photo-taking impairment effect）
   - 来源：<https://digitalcommons.fairfield.edu/psychology-facultypubs/26/>
   - 原句：Results showed a photo-taking-impairment effect: If participants took a photo of each object as a whole, they remembered fewer objects and remembered fewer details about the objects and the objects’ locations in the museum than if they instead only observed the objects and did not photograph them.
5. 但放大特寫嚟影，記憶反而冇受損
   - 来源：<https://digitalcommons.fairfield.edu/psychology-facultypubs/26/>
   - 原句：However, when participants zoomed in to photograph a specific part of the object, their subsequent recognition and detail memory was not impaired, and, in fact, memory for features that were not zoomed in on was just as strong as memory for features that were zoomed in on.
6. 交叉印證：實驗喺 Fairfield 大學嘅 Bellarmine 美術館進行，對象係大學生，第二日考記憶
   - 来源：<https://www.businessinsider.com/taking-photos-may-harm-our-ability-to-make-memories-study-2013-12>
   - 原句：A group of university students were led on a tour at the Bellarmine Museum of Art at Fairfield University and asked to either photograph or try and remember objects on display.
7. 研究者嘅解釋：人將相機當成『外部記憶裝置』，大腦就唔再加工個對象
   - 来源：<https://www.bbc.com/future/article/20150901-are-you-taking-too-many-pictures>
   - 原句：“What I think is going on is that we treat the camera as a sort of external memory device,” Henkel says.
