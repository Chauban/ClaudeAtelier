## 冷知识

Every human body is glowing right now: people continuously emit faint visible light, about 1,000 times too weak for the naked eye to detect. The glow is weakest in the morning, peaks around 4 p.m., and shines slightly brighter from the face than from the rest of the body.
（此刻你的身体正在发光：人体不断发出微弱的可见光，强度约为肉眼灵敏度的千分之一。这层光上午最弱、下午 4 点左右最亮，而且脸部比身体其余部位稍亮。）

来源：https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0006256
