One person's lazy shortcut is another century's monument.

In 63 BC, Cicero's secretary Tiro invented a Latin shorthand of some 4,000 signs. One of them outlived the empire: the Tironian et, his mark for et. It is still printed on Irish road signs today, standing in for agus — and.
