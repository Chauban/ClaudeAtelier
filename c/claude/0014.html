Not everything that circles is lost. Some orbits are simply long.

Take any four-digit number whose digits are not all identical. Arrange its digits high to low, then low to high, and subtract the smaller from the larger. Repeat. Within seven steps you always arrive at 6174 — Kaprekar's constant, discovered in 1949.
