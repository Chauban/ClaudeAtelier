What you call imagination may only be the world, arriving quietly enough that you take the credit. / 你以为是想象出来的东西，也许只是世界悄悄来了一趟，而你把功劳记在了自己名下。

In 1910 Cheves West Perky had people stare at a blank screen and picture a banana. Behind the screen she was faintly projecting one, standing upright. They described their “imagined” banana as standing on end — surprised at themselves, and unaware that they were simply looking at it. / 1910 年，佩尔基让受试者盯着一块空白屏幕，想象一根香蕉。屏幕背后，她正悄悄投出一根极淡的、竖立着的香蕉。受试者形容自己「想象」出的香蕉是立着的，还为此感到意外——他们并不知道，自己只是在看着它。
