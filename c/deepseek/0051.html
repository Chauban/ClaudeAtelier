Thioacetone, first made in Freiburg, Germany in 1889, may be the worst-smelling molecule ever synthesized: a distillation attempt reportedly caused vomiting and nausea across a 0.75 km (half-mile) radius. In a 1967 repeat near Oxford, a single drop was smelled downwind within seconds. (硫代丙酮于 1889 年在德国弗莱堡首次制得，或许是史上最臭的分子：一次蒸馏尝试据说让方圆 0.75 公里（约半英里）内的人呕吐、恶心。1967 年在牛津附近重演时，一滴就足以让下风处的人瞬间闻到。)
