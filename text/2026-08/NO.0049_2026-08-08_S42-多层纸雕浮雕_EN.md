## 冷知识

A male sea sapphire (Sapphirina, a 2 mm copepod) stacks hexagonal guanine crystal plates under its skin — about 1.5 micrometres wide and 80 nanometres thick — separated by thin layers of cytoplasm. The hue is set by the spacing of those gaps, not by the crystals; and when light strikes at roughly 45 degrees the reflection shifts out of visible light into the ultraviolet, so the animal blinks out of existence in mid-swim.

雄性海蓝宝水蚤（Sapphirina，一种约 2 毫米长的桡足类）皮下叠着一层层六边形鸟嘌呤晶片，约 1.5 微米宽、80 纳米厚，之间夹着薄薄的细胞质。决定颜色的是夹层的间距，而不是晶片本身；当光线以约 45 度射入时，反射会移出可见光、进入紫外——这只小虫便在游动中当场消失。
