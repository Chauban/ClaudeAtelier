## 冷知识

On 15 September 1896 the Katy railroad staged a head-on collision of two surplus locomotives near Waco, Texas, purely as a publicity stunt. Admission was free; a round-trip excursion ticket cost $2, and some 40,000 people came. Both boilers exploded on impact and flying debris killed three spectators. William Crush, the agent who dreamed it up, was fired that evening and rehired the next morning. Scott Joplin set the whole thing to music.

1896年9月15日，美国 Katy 铁路（Missouri–Kansas–Texas）在得州韦科以北把两台退役机车对开相撞，纯为宣传。入场免费，往返车票两美元，来了约四万人。撞击瞬间两台锅炉同时爆炸，飞溅的碎铁当场打死三名观众。策划人 William Crush 当晚被解雇，第二天早上又被请了回去。斯科特·乔普林随后为这场事故写了一首《Great Crush Collision March》。
