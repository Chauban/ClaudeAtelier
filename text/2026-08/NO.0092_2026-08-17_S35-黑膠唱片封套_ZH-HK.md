## 冷知识

巴黎十九區塞呂里耶大道的地底，有一個一九二一年就已建成的地鐵站 Haxo。月台、隧道全部完工，卻連一道通往街面的出入口都不曾動工——營運商重新核算，認為這條聯絡支線客量太少、不敷成本，在開通前便叫停。一百年來未曾有一名乘客在此下車；今天要進去，唯一的辦法是坐一列不會為它停站的列車經過。

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haxo_station

## 引用

- 建成年份與位置：英文維基「Ghost stations of the Paris Metro」——「A special tunnel, the voie des Fêtes, links Place des Fêtes to Porte des Lilas with an intermediary station called Haxo, constructed in 1921.」法文報導亦記其位於十九區 boulevard Sérurier 之下。<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghost_stations_of_the_Paris_Metro>
- 從未載客、無地面出入口：英文維基「Haxo station」——「Service trains have never called at Haxo, and no pedestrian access to street level was ever constructed.」<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haxo_station>
- 叫停理由（客量不足、不敷成本）：同上——「the railway operator, Compagnie du chemin de fer métropolitain de Paris, did not consider a service to be sufficiently profitable and thus halted the extension process」。
- 唯一進入方式：英文維基「Ghost stations of the Paris Metro」——「Two stations ... have no pedestrian access apart from another train: Porte Molitor and Haxo. Only during rare special service can these stations be visited.」
