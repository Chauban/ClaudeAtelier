## 金句

（没有人翻越那条规矩。他们只是把它一路读到了最后一行。）

## 冷知识

Boston capped its buildings at 125 feet. In 1915 a 496-foot tower opened downtown anyway: Peabody & Stearns had set it on top of the 1849 Custom House, and that site was federal ground, outside the city's rules. It remained the tallest building in Boston until 1964.

（波士顿曾把楼高限制在 125 英尺。1915 年，市中心却立起一座 496 英尺的塔楼——皮博迪与斯特恩斯事务所把它直接架在 1849 年的海关大楼顶上，而那块地属于联邦，不受市政法规管辖。此后它一直是波士顿第一高楼，直到 1964 年。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Custom_House_Tower

## 引用

- 高度限制与联邦豁免：搜索结果复述「Boston had a 125 foot limit on skyscrapers in the city, but as a federal building the Custom House Tower was exempt」，并称其获准升至 495 英尺——卡面取维基条目的成品高度 496 英尺（151 米）。
- 建造年代与建筑师：「The tower was designed by Peabody and Stearns and was added between 1913 and 1915」；原楼为 Ammi Burnham Young 设计的希腊复兴式海关大楼，1849 年落成。卡面写「the 1849 Custom House」。
- 加建方式：「Rather than demolishing the old building, they simply added a 32-story skyscraper on top of it」，且「The equally handsome tower, replac[ed] the original dome」——原有穹顶被拆除，塔楼从旧楼顶上长出。
- 保持最高纪录至 1964 年：「The Prudential Tower ended the Custom House Tower's 59-year reign as the tallest building in Boston when it was completed in 1964」，与「Boston's tallest building from 1915 to 1964」相互印证。
- 交叉印证来源：英文维基 Custom House Tower 条目、Boston Magazine 2016 年报道、aviewoncities 的建筑条目，三处对高度、年份、建筑师的说法一致。
- 未采信项：495 与 496 英尺两个数字并存（前者多指获批高度，后者为成品高度），卡面只写 496 并以「a 496-foot tower」表述，不另作解释。
