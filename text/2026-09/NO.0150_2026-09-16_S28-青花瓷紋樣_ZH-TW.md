## 金句

> 鄉音不是你留在故鄉的東西，是故鄉留在你身上的東西。

## 冷知识

裸鼴鼠的「輕啾」帶有群落口音。二〇二一年《科學》期刊分析了德國與南非實驗室裡七個群落、一百六十六隻裸鼴鼠的三萬六千多聲輕啾：牠們熱切回應同鄉的腔調，對外地口音卻幾乎不理。這口音是學來的——幼鼠送去別窩寄養，學的就是那一家的腔；而定調的是鼠后。鼠后一死，整窩的輕啾便逐漸走樣，直到新鼠后即位才重新對齊。

来源：https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.abc6588

## 引用

- 论文：Barker A. J., Veviurko G., Bennett N. C., Hart D. W., Mograby L., Lewin G. R., "Cultural transmission of vocal dialect in the naked mole-rat", *Science* 371(6528), 503–507, 2021（DOI: 10.1126/science.abc6588）。同期另有 Science 视角文章 "Colony-specific dialects of naked mole-rats"（DOI: 10.1126/science.abf7962）。
- 断言一：裸鼹鼠的 soft chirp（轻啾）带有群落特异的方言。原句（Science 编辑摘要）："a colony-specific greeting—the soft chirp—that is learned in early life and facilitates recognition of colony members"。
- 断言二：样本规模为七个群落、166 只、36,000 余声。原句（Science 新闻稿）："logged more than 36,000 of these chirps from 166 animals living in seven colonies in German and South African labs"。EurekAlert 与 Max Delbrück Center 新闻稿独立复述同一数字（"more than 36,000 individual recordings of soft chirps from animals housed in separate colonies"）。
- 断言三：裸鼹鼠热切回应本群落口音、忽略外来口音。原句：they reply "to the sounds of their own colony with frequent chirping, but largely ignoring foreign dialects"。
- 断言四：方言是后天习得的，交叉寄养幼鼠学养群口音。原句："Cross-fostered pups adopt their rearing colony's dialects"；另有 "The younger a pup was at the time it was adopted, the more closely it matched its new home's chirp"。
- 断言五：口音由鼠后定调；鼠后死后方言涣散，新鼠后即位后重新趋同。原句（Science 新闻）："one colony's chirps began to vary in the interregnum after their queen died, only to fall in line with a new monarch's dialect when she took the throne"；"Without the queen, the colony dialect became increasingly unrecognizable, with individual mole-rats demonstrating more variability in their soft chirp features"。
- 交叉印证：Science 官方（science.org 新闻与论文页）、Max Delbrück Center 机构新闻稿、Science News 与 Smithsonian Magazine 报道，就样本数、鼠后定调、交叉寄养三项结论彼此一致。
- 备注：卡面写「二〇二一年《科學》期刊」，未点名作者与机构；"三萬六千多聲" 对应原文 "more than 36,000"。
